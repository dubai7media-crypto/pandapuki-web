For parents

Panda Puki and Family is a Turkish-language cartoon channel for children aged 2–6. Our goal is to make the short time your child spends in front of a screen calm, warm and meaningful.

Our content principles

  • Calm pace: no rapid cuts, flashing lights or shouting.
  • No violence, fear or mockery; problems are solved by talking and working together.
  • Clear, correct Turkish with short sentences and repeated phrases, great for little learners.
  • One clear topic per episode: a feeling or a habit.
  • The channel is marked “made for kids” on YouTube.

Tips for watching together

⏱️

Keep it short

Our episodes are short. Less than one hour of screen time a day is recommended for ages 2–5; stay close by when you can.

💬

Talk afterwards

Bring the topic into everyday life with questions like “Why was Puki sad? What would you do?”

🎶

Sing together

You can sing the songs without a screen too: while brushing teeth, tidying up or going to bed.

📺

YouTube Kids

For little ones, you may prefer the YouTube Kids app, which has stricter ad and recommendation settings.

Privacy

This site does not collect any personal information from children or parents; there are no accounts, forms, ads or tracking cookies. Videos are shown in YouTube's “privacy-enhanced mode” (youtube-nocookie.com); when you play a video, YouTube's own privacy policy applies. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.
